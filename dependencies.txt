This repo holds ONE module. It clones, installs and runs on its own.
The machine-readable version of this file is `module.json`. The backend reads
it for /v1/deps; if you edit one, edit both.


WHAT THIS MODULE NEEDS (upstream)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nothing is required to START. A talk needs something to talk about, though.

  MODULE  WHAT WE USE IT FOR                   IF MISSING
  ------  ----------------------------------   ----------------------------------
  M06     the interviews a talk covers: the    No talk is made ("M06 is not
          student's history and each           answering"); one is owed and made
          interview's report                   when M06 is back.

  M04     the profile (name, weekly goal,      The talk is about the interview
          drives), mastery and trajectory      alone: no goal, drive or trend.

  M11     the counselor's face and voice, in   The browser's voice, or the talk
          the browser (@ai/core)               as text. The words are the same.

Every call to M06 and M04 forwards the student's identity, so those modules
apply their own rule about what M16 may read.


WHO USES THIS MODULE (downstream)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Platform_Shell   mounts `m16Routes` (/counsel), and shows `CounselNudge` on
                   Today and over the notes once an interview has ended.


THE FACES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The four counselors' heads are made by M11's scripts/make_faces.py from the
entries in its scripts/faces/portraits.json (counsel_kabir, counsel_tara,
counsel_sunita, counsel_prakash), and their voices are cast in M11's
voices/cast.json. All four are AI-generated and labelled so; none is a real
person. M11's tests read counselors.json here to hold the cast to it.
